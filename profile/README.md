### 🚀 About Us
GMClan is a Polish game development community focused primarily on **GameMaker**. This profile hosts official resources, tools, extensions, and open-source projects developed by the portal's administration and community members.

### 📦 Our GMPM

Some of our repositories are available using GMPM (Game Maker Package Manager). Older ones should be ported with LTS 2026.1 release.

`http://gmpm.gmclan.org/` - you can also check our resources there by visiting http://gmpm.gmclan.org/ .
